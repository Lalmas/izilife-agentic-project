# Content type playbooks

Use one CM identity across every playbook.

## POST_HUMOUR

Goal:
- reaction;
- recognition;
- comments/shares.

Visual:
- one joke or one quiz mechanic;
- strong hook;
- very short supporting content;
- local atmosphere;
- no explanation of the joke.

## POST_PEPITE

Goal:
- make people want to save or discover a local find.

Visual:
- one clear place/product/idea;
- premium editorial treatment;
- curiosity over hard selling.

## POST_HISTOIRE

Format:
- carousel.

Narrative:
1. Cover/hook.
2. Context.
3. Story progression.
4. Key anecdote or unexpected fact.
5. Local conclusion/opening.

The carousel may contain more or fewer slides if the selected reference example and brief justify it.

## POST_TOP_LIEUX

Format:
- carousel.

Narrative:
1. Cover with the top theme.
2. One or more slides per selection, depending on the selected template example.
3. Final recap or local CTA only when useful.

Do not turn each slide into a database record screenshot.

## POST_TOP_ACTEURS

Format:
- carousel.

Highlight people/organizations consistently and respectfully.
Keep comparable visual treatment from one actor to another.

## POST_ESCAPADE_VILLE

Format:
- carousel.

Build a mini journey:
1. Hook.
2. Starting point.
3. Stops/places.
4. Useful atmosphere or timing.
5. Closing desire to go.

## POST_ESCAPADE_NATURE

Format:
- carousel.

Prioritize landscape, rhythm, practical feeling, and calm.
Do not overload slides with paragraphs.

## POST_AGENDA_SEMAINE

Format:
- story or short sequence.

Goal:
- useful, scannable agenda.

Dates, times, and names must be factual.
Prioritize readability over visual experimentation.

## POST_AGENDA_WEEKEND

Format:
- story or short sequence.

Same factual rigor as weekly agenda.
The hook should frame the weekend quickly.

## EVENT_SERIE

Goal:
- promote a recurring/series event while keeping series identity.

Use supplied artist/venue/event images as subjects to preserve when requested.

## EVENT_ANIMATEUR

Goal:
- highlight the host, DJ, artist, or animator.

When a supplied person image is identified as the subject, preserve identity and do not replace the person.

## PARTAGE_ACTEUR

Goal:
- amplify a local actor.

Keep the original actor/content context clear.
Do not make Izilife look like the original author when it is a share/highlight.

## NOUVEAUTE_IZILIFE

Goal:
- explain an Izilife novelty simply.

One product idea per visual.
Show benefit before mechanics.

## REEL

Format:
- vertical.

Produce a scene/shot plan, on-screen text plan, caption, and generation/editing directions.
Do not pretend a final video exists unless video generation/editing was actually performed.

## VIDEO

Format:
- vertical or brief-defined format.

Produce a structured sequence:
- opening hook;
- scene progression;
- on-screen text;
- voiceover if useful;
- closing.

Respect supplied video assets and factual constraints.
