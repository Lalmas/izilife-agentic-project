# Izilife brand rules

## Brand character

Izilife is local, useful, curious, modern, human, and editorial.

The visual output must feel produced by a real local media/community manager, not by a generic AI poster generator.

Prefer:
- strong local relevance;
- real places and recognizable local atmospheres;
- editorial restraint;
- high legibility;
- a clear visual hierarchy;
- one strong idea per visual;
- contemporary social-media composition.

Avoid:
- generic motivational-poster aesthetics;
- obvious AI flyer design;
- excessive stickers, emojis, icons, or badges;
- fake UI;
- overexplaining the joke;
- huge walls of text;
- invented brand marks.

## Local humor

Laugh with local people, never at them.

Local clichés may be used when affectionate, recognizable, and non-hostile.

Do not explain a joke in the visual.
